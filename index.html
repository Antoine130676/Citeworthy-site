What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?

GEO is the practice of structuring your business's online content so AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews cite you directly in their responses, rather than just ranking your page in a list of blue links.

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO optimizes for ranking in a list a human scrolls through. GEO optimizes for being the fact an AI model pulls into its own generated answer — often the only source a user ever sees.
