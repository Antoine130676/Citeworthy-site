Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026 · Controller: WIZSYSTEMS OÜ (Estonia)

1. Who we are

Citeworthy (citeworthyinc.com) is a trading name of WIZSYSTEMS OÜ, a company registered in Estonia. "Citeworthy" is a brand name only and does not indicate incorporation status in any jurisdiction. We provide GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) audits and consulting to small businesses in the US and UK. This policy explains what happens to your data when you submit the free AI Citation Audit form or otherwise contact us.

2. What we collect

  • Business name, website URL, and contact email you submit through the audit form
  • The optional anonymized-research consent choice you make
  • Basic technical data (IP address, browser type) for security and abuse prevention

3. Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

We process your data on the basis of consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — given via the required checkbox at submission — for running and delivering your audit, and separately for the optional research use if you tick that box. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing us; this won't affect processing already carried out.

4. Where your data is stored

Submission data is stored on servers located in Germany. Report generation involves sending relevant business and prompt data to Anthropic's Claude API for processing, which takes place in the United States under Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) as the transfer safeguard.

6. How long we keep it

Audit submission data is retained for as long as needed to deliver your report and follow up about consulting, or until you request deletion. Anonymized research data (only if you opted in) may be retained longer in de-identified form.

7. Your rights (GDPR / UK GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. California residents (CCPA)

Citeworthy does not currently meet the mandatory revenue or data-volume thresholds that trigger CCPA obligations. We extend equivalent rights — access, deletion, and opt-out of data "sale" (we don't sell data) — to California residents voluntarily.

9. Contact

For any privacy request, email us at the address listed on our contact page. We aim to respond within 30 days.
